# Decision 2026-09-12: Q3 Enterprise Discount Cap (12%)

## Decision Record (Approved 2026-09-12)
**Status at Authoring:** Approved by Commercial Leadership on September 12, 2026.

To protect net dollar retention and prevent ad-hoc end-of-quarter discounting on mid-market logos, Sales and RevOps adopt a flat 12% ceiling on annual and multi-year enterprise quotes. Regional VPs may approve up to 12% inside CPQ; requests between 12% and 15% require Deal Desk Committee review during Tuesday office hours.

## Context & Rationale
In July and August, three 300-seat contracts closed with stacked promotional discounts (annual prepay + multi-year + pilot credit) that pushed effective discounts to 21%. Because our self-hosted and cloud agent compute margins were still stabilizing prior to SQLite WAL batch optimizations, preserving >84% blended gross margin was prioritized over multi-year TCV expansion.

## Rules Adopted
1. **Tier 1 (0%-8%):** AE self-serve inside CPQ for 12-month upfront commitments.
2. **Tier 2 (8%-12%):** Regional Sales Director sign-off for 24-month contracts over $100k ARR.
3. **Hard Ceiling (>12%):** Not permitted under standard playbooks; quotes must be restructured around phased seat rollouts rather than unit price reductions.
4. **Onboarding Fees:** Mandatory $15k platform onboarding fee for deployments exceeding 500 seats.

## Implementation Notes
- Updated CPQ validation rule `RULE_ENT_DISCOUNT_MAX_12` on September 13.
- Communicated in #revops-announcements and linked from Q3 sales enablement deck.
- Quarterly review scheduled for early Q4 once enterprise pipeline composition matures and larger 1,000+ seat opportunities enter procurement.

## Dissenting Perspectives Logged
Enterprise AEs noted that Fortune 1000 procurement teams routinely benchmark multi-year enterprise deals at 15%-20% off list price and warned this ceiling could stall Q4 whale accounts if not revisited once gross margin improves above 85%. RevOps agreed to re-evaluate once cloud compute COGS drop below $4.50/seat/month.
