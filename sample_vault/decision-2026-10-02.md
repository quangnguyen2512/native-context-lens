# Decision 2026-10-02: Enterprise 3-Year Floor Raised to 18%

## Supersession Notice
This decision **supersedes [[decision-2026-09-12]]** effective Q4 2026. The September 12 cap (12% ceiling) is no longer operative for strategic 1,000+ seat multi-year contracts.

## Revised Policy Summary
Following September infrastructure efficiency gains (38% drop in hosted agent token & compute COGS per seat), leadership approved a new Strategic Enterprise Tier allowing up to **18% blended discount** on non-cancellable 36-month agreements exceeding $350k ARR with annual upfront invoicing.

## Blocking Dependency
Although commercial leadership aligned on the 18% strategic floor during the October 2 executive sync, CPQ cannot unlock the new band and Northwind's order form cannot go out until we receive formal treasury & margin approval tracked in [[finance-sign-off]].

## Detailed Guardrails
- **Minimum Term:** 36 months non-cancellable, billed annually in advance (Net 45 max).
- **Minimum Floor ARR:** $350,000 annualized run rate by Month 12.
- **Renewal Cap:** Maximum 5% annual CPI uplift upon Year 4 renewal.
- **Gross Margin Floor:** Must remain >= 85.0% modeled over full contract lifetime including support overhead.
- **Non-Stacking Rule:** Promotional pilot credits cannot stack on top of the 18% strategic floor.

## Downstream Impact
- Unblocks [[pricing-review]] and immediately allows Deal Desk to issue Northwind Order Form #NW-2026-04 once [[finance-sign-off]] is signed.
- Requires CPQ rule `RULE_ENT_DISCOUNT_MAX_12` to be migrated to `RULE_ENT_STRATEGIC_18` with automated CFO notification audit logs.
- Sales enablement will publish updated battlecards after finance clears the final board-reporting ARR waterfall.
