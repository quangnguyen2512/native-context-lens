# Vendor DPA Template 2026-Q2 (Standard 72h Notice)

## Standard Terms (Approved 2026-06-15)
This template served as Native's standard subprocessor Data Processing Agreement during Q2 2026 for seed-stage infrastructure vendors.

## Core Clauses
1. **Breach Notification:** Subprocessor notifies Native within 72 hours of confirming a personal data incident.
2. **Audit Rights:** Annual third-party SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 summary certificate satisfies customer audit requests.
3. **Liability Cap:** Standard 12-month fees paid cap with mutual exclusion of consequential damages.
4. **Data Transfer Mechanism:** Incorporates 2021 EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Module Three: Processor-to-Processor) with standard Annex I & II technical measures.
5. **Sub-subprocessors:** 15-day advance email notice prior to onboarding downstream hosting vendors.

## Historical Usage Notes
Used cleanly across initial US infrastructure providers in May-July 2026. Several European enterprise prospects later noted that their own MSAs require 24-hour upstream breach notice and super-cap indemnification for confidentiality breaches, prompting Legal to review whether Q2 terms remain adequate for Tier-1 storage vendors.
